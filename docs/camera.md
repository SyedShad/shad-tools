# Record your screen and front camera

Open **Record Screen with Camera** in Shad Capture or the Shad Tools palette. Choose **Load screens and windows**, then select a display or a window. Choose the front camera and leave **Microphone** on to include your voice. The microphone follows the Mac’s current default when recording starts.

Choose **Circle**, **Square**, or **Rounded square**. Under **Background**, choose **Remove green screen** for a physical green backdrop or **Person cutout** for on-device person segmentation. **Original** keeps your camera background. Adjust the size from 10% to 60% of the screen’s shorter edge, drag the preview to position it, or choose a corner. **Mirror** flips the camera image horizontally.

Click **Start recording**, choose an MP4 destination, and allow camera and microphone access when prompted. macOS may also ask for Screen Recording permission for the Shad app you opened. The recorder window hides, a click-through camera bubble appears over the source, and a red **REC** control appears in the menu bar. Click that control to stop and save. Reopen **Record Screen with Camera** during recording to see the live composition or adjust the camera layout. The recorder’s own windows are excluded from display captures.

The output is a standard H.264 MP4 with the camera composited into the frames and optional AAC microphone audio. The longest side is capped at 1920 pixels. Layout changes during recording appear in the exported video. For editable separate webcam tracks, area selection, system audio and Studio timeline editing, use the MacShot recording commands in the same Capture tool.
